When to buy (Accumulation Zones)

Market bottoms and panic selling

1. Steam Sales & Operations: When Valve releases a new pass or sale, users sell cheap cases to buy new content. Prices drop by 15%-30%.

2. Oversupply cycle: The ideal buying point for cases in active circulation is when the massive post-launch drop curve flattens out completely (month 6 onwards).

When to sell (Profit Taking)

All-Time Highs (ATH) & Hype

1. Moving to the "Rare Pool": When a case is discontinued from active rotation, its drop rate falls to 0.03%. Its value immediately spikes due to the drastic supply cut.

2. Big Update Hype: Massive content patches bring back nostalgic players who buy keys in bulk to open old cases, temporarily boosting their value.

Redemption Strategy (Skins vs Cases)

A common mistake is spending credits on random collections looking for a red skin. Historical math proves that playing it safe generates higher continuous returns:

  • **Armory Cases:** Low credit cost, high immediate liquidity.
  • **Charms:** Profitability depends on the initial level of hype.
  • **Limited Edition Skins:** Hold them long-term without applying stickers.
XP per Hour Optimization

Credits are unlocked by earning raw experience. The most optimal way to farm these credits without wasting time in long competitive matches is by playing intensely in fast game modes (like **Arms Race / Massive Deathmatch**) during days with an active weekly XP bonus.
